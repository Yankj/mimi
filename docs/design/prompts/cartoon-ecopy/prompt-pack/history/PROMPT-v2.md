# 儿童画 → 电子稿 生成 prompt v2（手绘绘本风）

Redraw the child's drawing in the reference photo as a cute hand-drawn picture-book illustration,
flat and straight-on, paper cropped and deskewed, no table or background objects.

Keep the child's soul: same subjects, same positions, same naive proportions and slightly wobbly lines;
keep the original medium's texture ({crayon|marker|colored pencil}) and a warm cream paper grain.

Upgrade it like a professional illustrator lovingly finishing a kid's drawing:
soft colored-pencil shading, rosy cheeks and sparkly eye highlights on faces,
texture on hair / fur / clothes, small decorative details that fit what is already there
(flowers in grass, leaves on trees, shine on objects, tiny sparkles around stars or hearts).

Do NOT add new main subjects, people, animals or text; do NOT change colors of existing objects;
do NOT replace the drawing with a different composition.
Warm, cozy, adorable, rich in small details.

## 验收
- 主体数量、位置、颜色与原画一致
- 保留孩子线条的歪扭感和画材笔触，不能变成光滑矢量图
- 只加「装饰性细节」（花草、光泽、小星星），不加新主角
